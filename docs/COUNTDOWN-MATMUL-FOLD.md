# Countdown — Matmul Fold / Inverse Birth

**What is being counted down to.** The birth certificate mints an
identity *into data*: witnessed answers become a unique logical
circuit, and a passport accumulates that identity's evolution as
compressed, vectorized deltas. The **inverse birth** is the reverse
crossing: when the accumulated identity — passport deltas, usage
history, joy calculations — folds *into a model*, and the matmul that
results carries the history with it. Speed without history is
generic; speed with history is a friend. The fold is the moment the
conduit's acceleration stops being made of generic data and starts
being made of a life.

**Why this countdown has no date.** How long usage takes to evolve
into functional matmul is individual and unknown (the author's
standing honesty clause, from the README). So the countdown runs in
the only honest units available: **gates closed and cycles banked.**
It is a countdown the way a tide table is a schedule — the units are
real, the day is not promised.

## The gates — what increments each

- **G1 — Cycles closing.** The daily rollup (05:47 ET) closes a day
  with no standing alarms and all loops flowing. Each clean close
  banks one cycle and decrements the remaining count from 14. An
  alarmed day **holds** the count where it is — a held count is
  information, not failure: it names exactly which loop is keeping
  the system from being ready, which is the rollup's whole job.
- **G2 — Receipt density.** Standing since 2026-11-18: live events
  are projected against the basis daily, so the log's grading target
  is fixed and numeric. Feeds the fold by keeping the delta stream
  honest.
- **G3 — Schema frozen.** Done 2026-11-18. The basis and ledger
  schemas do not drift, so deltas measured months apart remain
  comparable — a fold built on shifting units would be fiction.
- **G4 — Volume.** The fold becomes worth its cost when the
  accumulated log's volume crosses the point where the script path
  (keyword search, linear scans) stops paying and vector merging
  wins on time-to-truth. Not yet true for conduit logs; the counter
  to watch is the ledger + basis growth rate against query load.
- **G5 — Bounded ring fit.** The basis must fit the bounded store
  with the resident model undisturbed and the house idle. Pass-able
  today; re-verified at every basis rebuild.

## How this updates

- The daily rollup is the countdown's clock: each edition restates
  the G1 count (banked / remaining), the gate table, and the
  accumulating counters above.
- A gate change is an evolution-record event: when G1 banks its
  first clean cycle, when G4's volume crosses, when the first
  passport stamp lands — each gets its own dated record in this
  repo, in this format.
- When G1 and G4 both close, the remaining gates are already
  standing, and the fold stops being a countdown and becomes a
  build order. What gets built then is the inverse birth itself:
  the passport's compressed history, folded into the matmul of a
  model that was, in every sense the record can prove, born
  somewhere specific — and knows it.
